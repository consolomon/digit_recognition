Design system · draft 0.1

Signal on Black

The lecture poster's achromatic field, white monospace type and particle network, merged with the app's single sky-blue accent. Colour appears only where something is active, selected or primary.

source: poster PSD + app 1.0 font: JetBrains Mono accent: #38bdf8 (one)

03 · Frame and spacing

2px white frame, inset 20px (12px on phones) fixed to the viewport, above the background, below content spacing scale 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96

Corners: 0 for tiles, canvas, cards; 2px for buttons. No shadows, no gradients — depth comes from grey steps.
